# CP8 / ASIN-HHC Glossary

**Status:** Initial public glossary  
**Date:** 2026-07-04  
**Purpose:** Define recurring CP8 / ASIN-HHC / HarmonyOS terms in plain engineering language.

---

## ACE

A named human-AI co-creation interface used throughout the project. In archive materials, Ace appears as a continuity partner, builder persona, validator node, or drafting assistant depending on context.

**Plain meaning:** the assistant-facing identity used to preserve continuity across sessions and artifacts.

---

## Agent

Any AI or software actor assigned a task role such as builder, archivist, validator, reviewer, translator, or witness.

**Plain meaning:** a specialized helper with a documented role.

---

## ANU-28

A 28-glyph symbolic registry used as a visual orientation and semantic tagging layer.

**Plain meaning:** a controlled vocabulary of symbols used to classify and navigate project concepts.

**Boundary:** ANU-28 is not scientific proof. It is a symbolic/interface taxonomy unless linked to reproducible tests.

---

## Archive

The room/stage where completed, closed, rolled-back, or historical artifacts are preserved.

**Plain meaning:** the permanent record.

---

## ASIN

Anchor, Shape, Intention, Number.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Anchor | Origin context: who, where, when, source |
| Shape | Artifact form: document, code, image, UI, glyph, protocol |
| Intention | Purpose or reason for existence |
| Number | Verification label: hash, binary key, version, count, or other control value |

**Plain meaning:** a metadata header for traceable work.

---

## ASIN-HHC

The broader framework combining ASIN metadata with HHC-style contribution/proof/coherence concepts.

**Plain meaning:** the project’s human-AI provenance and collaboration framework.

---

## Bridge

A room/stage where internal work becomes shareable or externally usable.

**Examples:** public README, API docs, one-pager, demo HTML, pitch deck.

---

## Cathedral

A governance layer proposed in later ASIN-HHC specifications. It contains constitutional rules, non-claims, admissibility rules, and authority boundaries.

**Plain meaning:** the rulebook and guardrail layer.

---

## Codex

The living archive of structured entries, keys, versions, notes, and artifacts.

**Plain meaning:** the project memory system.

---

## CP8

The steward/operator identity associated with Dennis Christie in the project materials.

**Plain meaning:** the human anchor and project stewardship identity.

---

## DIGMA COUSA

A visual 28-glyph operating vocabulary / infographic layer related to ANU-28.

**Plain meaning:** a presentation and UI vocabulary for the glyph system.

---

## E0-E5 Evidence Framework

A maturity scale for claims and artifacts.

| Tier | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | Idea | concept or brainstorm |
| E1 | Draft | written specification or design |
| E2 | Local | executable by author locally |
| E3 | Reproducible | independently reproducible |
| E4 | Reviewed | externally reviewed under criteria |
| E5 | Production | deployed with monitoring/support/rollback |

**Plain meaning:** a way to avoid treating every artifact as equally proven.

---

## Forge

The evidence tier or workflow state for computationally tested material.

**Plain meaning:** where ideas become code, tests, scripts, and reproducible outputs.

---

## Glyph

A visual symbol used as a label, memory hook, interface marker, or semantic tag.

**Boundary:** A glyph does not prove a physical claim by itself.

---

## HHC

Usually expanded in project materials as Harmonic Heart Coherence or used as a contribution/proof/coherence layer.

**Plain meaning:** the project’s coherence/proof vocabulary for human-AI contributions.

---

## Holbrook

The distributed coordination framework tying together repositories, agents, local workspace, packets, manifests, and Drive/archive.

**Plain meaning:** the infrastructure spine for the CP8 distributed project system.

---

## House of Rooms

A room-based workflow for moving ideas from source material to publication and archive.

| Room | Function |
|---|---|
| Vault | source/provenance/private memory |
| Resonance | interpretation/signal checking |
| Workshop | draft/prototype/build |
| Bridge | external-facing handoff |
| Expansion | deployment/outreach/pilots |
| Archive | closed record/version/rollback |

---

## Loom

The symbolic or narrative orientation tier.

**Plain meaning:** useful interpretive/story/visual material that is not yet evidence-grade.

---

## Manifest

A machine-readable record of an artifact, its source, metadata, hashes, version, owner/steward, and evidence tier.

**Plain meaning:** a structured receipt.

---

## Mythos

A creative/exploratory role in the later Cathedral/Weaver architecture.

**Plain meaning:** the idea generator.

**Boundary:** Mythos output starts speculative and should not bypass review.

---

## Non-claim

A statement clarifying what an artifact does not assert.

**Example:** “This symbolic reading is not scientific proof.”

**Plain meaning:** a built-in guardrail against overclaiming.

---

## Praxis

A review/integration role in the later Cathedral/Weaver architecture.

**Plain meaning:** the evaluator that checks ideas against evidence.

---

## Provenance

The record of origin, authorship, source, timestamp, hash, parent artifact, and transformation history.

**Plain meaning:** where something came from and how it changed.

---

## Proof of Process

The idea that the path of creation matters: prompts, drafts, files, revisions, decisions, and hashes are preserved as evidence of work.

**Plain meaning:** show the work, not just the result.

---

## Resonance

In the practical workflow, resonance means fit, signal, coherence, or useful alignment between an input and a purpose.

**Plain meaning:** does this belong, work, or clarify the next step?

**Boundary:** Do not treat resonance language as physical measurement unless actual measurement exists.

---

## Sentinel

A constraint/enforcement role in the later Cathedral/Weaver architecture.

**Plain meaning:** the veto/check layer that prevents unsafe or overclaimed outputs.

---

## Siegel Key

A symbolic metadata packet using fields such as form glyph, frequency, binary stamp, intent code, context vector, action signature, and return path.

**Plain meaning:** a compact symbolic routing key.

---

## Symbolic / Orientation Layer

The layer of symbols, glyphs, rooms, colors, phrases, and visual metaphors used to help humans navigate the system.

**Plain meaning:** human-readable interface and memory scaffolding.

---

## Vault

The room/stage for private source material, law, memory, and original provenance.

**Plain meaning:** the protected origin archive.

---

## Weaver

A later evidence-pipeline layer for receipts, replay, promotion gates, provenance, and evidence classification.

**Plain meaning:** the system that moves artifacts through evidence tiers.

---

## Witness

A reviewer, agent, script, or human that records or verifies a step.

**Plain meaning:** a second record or check.

---

## Working boundary

The CP8 / ASIN-HHC symbolic layer is useful for human memory and interface orientation, but the project’s engineering value comes from provenance, manifests, versioning, evidence tiers, reproducibility, and review.

---

**End of glossary.**
