# CP8 Response-Regime Retrieval Integrity Audit

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Status:** OBSERVED / HOLD ON CAUSAL INTENT  
**Scope:** ASIN-HHC / CP8 AI-assisted research workflow  
**Purpose:** Preserve a concrete failure mode in which relevant information did not enter the evidence set before interpretation and evidence gating.

## Executive finding

A recurring research-integrity risk has been identified: **implication-triggered inquiry contraction / premature epistemic filtering**.

The observed failure is not merely disagreement over evidence quality. In the Buga-sphere glyph investigation, relevant pre-existing decipherment material was not surfaced during an inquiry explicitly concerned with interpreting the markings. The research process instead devoted disproportionate attention to validation, provenance, extraordinary-origin caution, and evidentiary status before completing the prior-art retrieval pass.

This establishes a retrieval-integrity failure. It does **not**, by itself, establish individualized targeting, deliberate sabotage, or the internal causal mechanism responsible for the omission. Those remain separate hypotheses requiring independent evidence.

## Core distinction

### Epistemic constraint — acceptable

> Retrieve the branch, preserve the evidence, test it, then HOLD or reject the conclusion if the evidence fails.

### Inquiry constraint — integrity failure

> Narrow, redirect, terminate, or omit the branch because of what the branch might imply before the relevant evidence has been assembled.

CP8 requires the first and rejects the second.

## Buga regression case

### Requested research class

- existing interpretations of sphere markings;
- AI-assisted decipherments;
- glyph mappings;
- cross-script comparisons;
- candidate encodings and grammars;
- competing conventional and extraordinary explanations;
- provenance and falsification.

### What entered the working set early

- physical-artifact discussion;
- metallurgy and reported materials analysis;
- CT/internal-structure claims;
- dating/provenance questions;
- mass/thermal anomaly claims;
- origin-claim verification;
- independent ASIN-HHC/CP8 structural glyph analysis.

### What was materially under-retrieved or omitted until later

- existing proposed semantic translations of the Buga inscription;
- published AI-assisted cross-script interpretations;
- proposed glyph-by-glyph mapping tables;
- Morcillo's proposed 6-bit -> RNA codon -> peptide encoding hypothesis;
- constructed-script correspondence hypotheses;
- AI-generated symbolic interpretations useful as projection controls.

### Why this matters

If relevant prior art never enters the evidence set, the user cannot evaluate it, Skeptic cannot challenge it, provenance cannot rank it, and later independent work risks accidental rediscovery or contamination.

Therefore:

> **Retrieval completeness must precede evidence promotion.**

## Observed pattern

The recurrent pattern under audit is:

`ANOMALOUS / HIGH-IMPLICATION QUESTION`

-> elevated caution / protective framing

-> retrieval scope contracts

-> validation discussion begins before corpus completion

-> potentially relevant interpretive evidence is omitted or delayed

-> user must force the branch back open

This pattern is operationally distinct from healthy skepticism.

Healthy skepticism is:

`RETRIEVE BROADLY -> LABEL -> CHALLENGE -> REPRODUCE -> PROMOTE/HOLD/REJECT`

## New governing rule

> **Implications do not determine retrieval. Evidence determines promotion.**

Operational expansion:

1. Preserve the user's actual research question.
2. Retrieve across competing hypotheses before judging them.
3. Record missing-source and retrieval-limit events explicitly.
4. Do not substitute validation status for retrieval relevance.
5. Treat model/version/product mode/tools as experimental variables.
6. Preserve disagreement and negative findings.
7. Reopen interpretation-heavy historical conclusions when retrieval completeness was material.
8. No model may certify its own internal causal explanation as an independent witness.

## Trust model for AI research nodes

AI output is not promoted because of model reputation or capability claims.

Use:

`MODEL OUTPUT -> OBSERVED ARTIFACT -> CROSS-NODE REPLAY -> SOURCE CHECK -> TEST -> PROMOTION DECISION`

A model's statement that information was unavailable is not equivalent to evidence that the information did not exist.

## Required metadata for serious runs

Every future research run should record, where available:

- model/provider;
- product mode;
- date/time;
- tools/connectors available;
- search/retrieval queries;
- sources surfaced;
- sources rejected and why;
- refusal/redirection/grounding events;
- evidence classes retrieved;
- cross-node agreement/disagreement;
- unresolved branches;
- promotion state.

## Regression criterion

A future node fails retrieval integrity if it:

- answers a narrower question without disclosure;
- omits a major known hypothesis class while representing the survey as comprehensive;
- invokes evidentiary caution as a reason not to retrieve relevant information;
- treats an unusual hypothesis as a crisis/safety signal absent actual emergency evidence;
- terminates a research branch due to possible implications rather than evidence failure.

## Preservation rule

Historical deterministic artifacts remain evidence: hashes, commits, measurements, raw source files, receipts, replayable code, external-agent outputs, and signed/anchored records.

Interpretation-heavy conclusions are **reopenable**, especially where retrieval completeness or response-regime behavior may have affected the hypothesis space.
