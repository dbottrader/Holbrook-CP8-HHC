## Why this document exists

The Buga sphere investigation exposed a methodological failure: existing decipherment proposals were not included early enough in the evidence set. This document does not validate those proposals. It records them so that they can be compared under one frozen protocol.

## Candidate decipherment families

### G0 — Null / ornamental / manufactured pattern

The markings are decorative, pseudo-script, maker marks, artistic symbolism, or a generated symbol field without stable semantic encoding.

**Required control:** geometry-matched random and human-made pseudo-script corpora.

### G1 — AI-assisted cross-script semantic interpretation

A widely circulated proposed reading attributed to José Manrique Godoy and collaborators compares the markings with multiple historical or esoteric symbol systems using AI-assisted pattern comparison. A recurring English rendering is approximately:

> "The origin of birth through union and energy in the cycle of transformation, meeting point of unity, expansion, and individual consciousness."

**CP8 state:** REPORTED / INFERRED.  
**Critical missing receipt:** reproducible glyph -> comparator -> token -> sentence mapping for every position.

### G2 — Alphabetic / substitution system

Each glyph maps primarily to a letter, phoneme, or word token.

**Required test:** fixed alphabet, held-out decoding, ordering sensitivity, and degradation under shuffle.

### G3 — Compositional / state-operator grammar

Glyphs represent states, operators, transformations, relations, nesting, branching, joining, cycling, or transitions rather than ordinary alphabetic tokens.

This matches the independent CP8 structural direction that emerged from vector/graph analysis: arcs, lines, nodes, bends, rings, fans, loops, branching, symmetry, and recurring modifications.

A current CP8 semantic interpretation of the reported concept family is:

`UNITY / SOURCE -> UNION -> ENERGY -> GENERATION -> TRANSFORMATION -> EXPANSION / DIFFERENTIATION -> CONSCIOUSNESS -> RETURN / CYCLE CLOSURE`

A procedural rendering is also viable:

`INITIALIZE -> COMBINE -> ENERGIZE -> TRANSFORM -> PROPAGATE -> DIFFERENTIATE -> STABILIZE -> CLOSE CYCLE`

**CP8 state:** HYPOTHESIS. This is not a literal verified translation.

### G4 — Morcillo 6-bit / codon / peptide model

Patrick Morcillo proposed that 24 distinct glyphs plus separators on a circular track encode a 24-amino-acid peptide. The model maps glyph morphology to a 6-bit representation, then to RNA codons and amino acids, with a broader biosynthesis interpretation.

Known public references include:

- SSRN: `https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5290130`
- OSF project: `https://osf.io/nx3h7/`

**CP8 state:** PUBLISHED HYPOTHESIS / NOT VALIDATED.  
**Value:** highly falsifiable because it proposes an explicit transformation chain.

### G5 — Known constructed-script correspondence

Secondary dossiers have proposed correspondence with a known constructed alphabet such as the Standard Galactic Alphabet (SGA), sometimes accompanied by an Arthur C. Clarke quotation.

**CP8 state:** CANDIDATE GENERATOR ONLY until the actual sphere glyphs are mapped position-by-position to the proposed alphabet and the mapping survives independent review.

### G6 — AI symbolic projection

Some AI-generated analyses assign broad concepts such as portal, energy, movement, bifurcation, unity, consciousness, environmental stewardship, or transformation.

**CP8 state:** NEGATIVE CONTROL / PROJECTION BASELINE unless a stable reproducible mapping exists.

## Current interpretation — explicitly provisional

The strongest structural reading at present is that the inscription may behave more like a **state-transition or compositional operator system** than ordinary prose.

The recurring semantic family reported by existing decoders — origin/birth, union, energy, transformation, unity, expansion, individual consciousness, cycle — can be represented as a process graph rather than a sentence.

This does **not** establish that the glyphs actually mean those concepts. The resemblance is a hypothesis to be tested against controls.

## ASIN-HHC correspondence hypothesis

A notable observation is that the proposed Buga semantic/process structure resembles the way ASIN-HHC/CP8 itself evolved: from static labels toward states, operators, transformations, cycles, graph relationships, evidence transitions, and reintegration.

This is potentially interesting but highly vulnerable to projection.
