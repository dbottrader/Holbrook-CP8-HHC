## Working storyline

Source differentiates. Difference creates relationship; relationship permits interaction; interaction produces changing states. Repeated transformations can generate stable patterns, boundaries, structures, and increasingly complex forms.

Under this model, creation is not required to mean absolute nothing becoming something. A competing formulation is that differentiated form emerges from an underlying state or source:

`UNITY → DIFFERENCE → RELATION → TRANSFORMATION → FORM`

This creates a recursive possibility:

`SOURCE → REALITY → LIFE → CONSCIOUSNESS → REALITY-REPRESENTATION`

The stronger ASIN-HHC hypothesis asks whether reality may function as a form of **self-representation**: underlying reality generating differentiated states through which information, relationship, and experience become possible.

This differs from the popular modern computer-simulation picture. The current hypothesis does **not** require an external programmer, an external computer, or a fake universe. It is closer to self-manifestation / self-instantiation / self-representation of an underlying source through temporary differentiated forms.
