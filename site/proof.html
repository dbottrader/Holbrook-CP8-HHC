Demo data. The gates, settlements, and receipt chain on this page are the local fixture powc-launch-v1. They are not live bounties. The app console reads project ecenvlwyenpakrxfuqup. When that project has no bounties, the console says so.

This demo sends an undistributed remainder back to the publisher. The live escrow keeps that remainder in the verifier pool: the last verifier id receives pool % n. The console ledger shows the live rule, and it labels this page as the demo.

Replay it locally

git clone https://github.com/dbottrader/Holbrook-CP8-HHC.git
cd Holbrook-CP8-HHC
python3 scripts/replay_proof_of_work_commons.py --out /tmp/powc-replay
python3 scripts/replay_proof_of_work_commons.py --check site/proof

The second command reruns the fixture and compares the bytes with the files published here. You can also open the raw files: replay.json, receipts.json, manifest.json.

Eight launch gates

  1. bounty_posted — a bounty has a publisher, a positive price, and a hash of its text.
  2. fenced_lease — a second worker is rejected while a lease is live, and may claim it after the lease ends.
  3. verifier_independence — two verifiers, distinct from each other, the worker, and the publisher.
  4. settlement_90_5_5 — an all-pass bounty of 10.000 HHC-SIM pays 9.000, 0.500, and 0.500.
  5. fail_full_refund — one fail refunds the publisher 4.000 HHC-SIM and pays a pass vote nothing.
  6. credit_conservation — every settlement adds back to the escrowed price, including a 7 milli-credit remainder.
  7. receipt_chain — every receipt hash recomputes and links to the previous receipt.
  8. hold_boundary — promotion stays HOLD, the public config is the anon key, and HHC in this fixture is a utility credit for verified contribution.
