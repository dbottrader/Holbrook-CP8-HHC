# CC-OS-AI-CROSS-EXAM-001 — Cross-Examination Against 2026 AI Discovery Capabilities

Status: EXECUTION / HOLD

## Purpose

Re-run the CC-OS research program using capabilities demonstrated by current scientific AI systems, while preventing the AI itself from becoming the source and validator of a claim.

The external AI developments are instruments. They do not constitute evidence for any crop-circle hypothesis.

## New capabilities that materially change the research design

### 1. Agentic hypothesis tournaments

Modern scientific AI systems can generate, critique, rank and evolve competing hypotheses rather than returning one interpretation. Co-Scientist demonstrated this architecture with Generation, Reflection, Ranking, Evolution, Proximity and Meta-review agents plus test-time compute scaling and experimental validation. [External capability reference: Nature 2026]

CC-OS adaptation:

- GENERATOR agents propose competing geometric programs.
- SKEPTIC agents attack them.
- NULL agent generates mundane/control explanations.
- FITTER scores reconstruction error and description length.
- PROVENANCE agent checks source contamination.
- REPLAY agent executes frozen candidates.
- META agent may summarize only after the lower-level receipts exist.

No agent may both originate and promote a hypothesis.

### 2. Hard-mode discovery

Recent theorem-proving research distinguishes answering a problem whose answer is implicitly supplied by its formal statement from genuinely discovering the answer first. This exposes a critical failure mode for CC decoding: a model can appear highly accurate when the desired interpretation is already embedded in the prompt.

CC-OS adaptation:

HARD MODE:
- Give the model only frozen geometry.
- No formation name.
- No date.
- No location.
- No known symbolic interpretation.
- No historical commentary.
- No candidate constants.
- No previously proposed answer.

The model must independently produce:
1. primitive graph,
2. candidate generative rule,
3. reconstruction,
4. confidence,
5. falsifier.

Only afterward may semantic information be revealed.

### 3. Geometry-aware multimodal reasoning

2026 multimodal research is explicitly addressing geometric insufficiency by giving models dedicated geometry channels and the ability to recognize when ordinary visual reasoning is inadequate.

CC-OS adaptation:

Do not ask an MLLM to infer geometry directly from the photograph when metric precision matters.

Instead provide:
IMAGE -> calibrated geometry extraction -> explicit coordinate/graph representation -> MLLM reasoning.

The image remains evidence. The geometric representation becomes the computational substrate.

### 4. Scientific self-critique is not independent confirmation

Current agentic-science systems show that recursive critique and test-time compute can improve hypothesis quality, but the agents remain correlated computational observers.

Therefore:

N agents agreeing != N independent confirmations.

Independence requires materially different measurement paths, source representations, models, or physical observations.

## Cross-examination battery

Every serious CC-OS candidate must pass:

### X1 — Blind reconstruction
Can the proposed grammar reconstruct the measured formation without semantic hints?

### X2 — Held-out prediction
Can parameters learned from one subset predict withheld structure?

### X3 — Null competition
Does the candidate outperform geometry-matched random and mundane construction models?

### X4 — Perturbation stability
Does the result survive reasonable uncertainty in center, scale, perspective and segmentation?

### X5 — Alternative segmentation
Does another defensible primitive decomposition produce the same underlying rule?

### X6 — Model-family independence
Does the signal survive different algorithmic families?

### X7 — Source independence
Does the result survive different photographs or diagrams of the same formation?

### X8 — Temporal transfer
Does a rule inferred from earlier formations predict structure in later formations without retrospective tuning?

### X9 — Cross-formation compression
Does one compact grammar explain multiple formations better than independent ad-hoc descriptions?

### X10 — Semantic quarantine
Does removing the proposed meaning leave the geometry result unchanged?

### X11 — Adversarial mundane explanation
Can ordinary construction, perspective, crop management, image processing, or selection bias reproduce the observed feature?

### X12 — Replay
Can another operator execute the frozen specification and obtain the same result?

## New CC-OS research principle

The strongest discovery is not:

"AI recognized a hidden symbol."

The stronger discovery is:

"AI independently discovered a compact generative rule from blinded geometry, that rule reconstructed withheld structure, survived null competition and independent replay, and only afterward produced a semantic interpretation."

That is the standard we will use.

## Promotion gate

A candidate may move:

HOLD -> OBSERVED -> EXECUTED -> REPRODUCED -> ESTABLISHED

only when the corresponding evidence exists.

AI novelty is a discovery aid, never a promotion criterion.
