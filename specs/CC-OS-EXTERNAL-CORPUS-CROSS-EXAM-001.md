# CC-OS External Corpus Cross-Examination

Node: CCFA-3382-CORPUS-CROSS-EXAM-001  
Status: HOLD / replay pending

## Observed report

A public project analyzes 3,382 vector diagrams from 1990-2020. It reports geometric extraction, preregistered hypotheses, null models, and a Phase 8 topology analysis.

Key reported findings:
- Complexity rises over time, reported rho 0.37.
- Six-fold symmetry rises from 2.5 percent in the 1990s to 10.1 percent in the 2010s.
- Median enclosed holes rise from 1 to 4 to 6 across the 1990s, 2000s, and 2010s.
- Phi ratios, Fibonacci counts at values >= 8, preferred constants, Zipf-like motif frequency, and higher-dimensional projection matching were not supported under the project's tests.
- The source is illustrator-produced vector diagrams, not field photographs or surveyed geometry.

## Cross-examination

The source PDFs are not redistributed, though a filename/size/hash manifest is provided. Public metadata needs reconciliation: headline 3,382 formations, catalog 3,387 plates, Phase 8 N=3,386; summary says 148 tests while Phase 8 says 161.

## New tests

1. Reconcile IDs and eligible N across catalog, descriptors, test log, and Phase 8.
2. Replay topology from per-formation outputs and test connectivity sensitivity.
3. Separate topology from primitive count, era, county, and book/illustrator effects.
4. Turn the documented degenerate-null, circular-feature, and ring-thickness-control failures into CC-OS regression tests.
5. Test grammar on held-out vector diagrams, then separately on rectified aerial images.

Inference: this is a diagram-domain benchmark, not a direct proxy for field geometry. Promotion remains HOLD until replay and count reconciliation.
