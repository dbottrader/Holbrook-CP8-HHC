# CC-DECODING-001 — Governed Crop-Circle Decoding Pipeline

Status: DRAFT / HOLD

Purpose: add crop-circle analysis as a governed evidence lane in Holbrook-CP8-HHC without allowing geometry, AI agreement, symbolic resemblance, or harmonic scores to become historical claims.

Pipeline:
1. SOURCE FREEZE — preserve original image/source URL, date, location, crop-circle identifier, photographer/provider, transformations, and SHA-256.
2. GEOMETRIC NORMALIZATION — perspective correction, scale/orientation registration, center estimation, ring/arc/line extraction, uncertainty bounds.
3. MORPHOLOGY — segment motifs into primitives and glyph-like components without semantic labels.
4. VECTOR/GRAPH REPRESENTATION — nodes, edges, curvature, radii, symmetry, topology, adjacency, repeated motifs.
5. DESCRIPTIVE STATISTICS — counts, entropy, compression, symmetry, repetition, spatial frequency, golden-angle/phyllotaxis tests where justified.
6. NULL CONTROLS — randomized, mirrored, rotated, perturbed, and geometry-matched controls. Pre-register hypotheses before significance testing.
7. MULTI-MODEL BLIND ANALYSIS — independent vision/geometry/LLM systems receive blinded inputs; record model/version/prompt/config and raw outputs.
8. CROSS-DOMAIN RETRIEVAL — only after blind feature extraction, compare against ancient glyphs, calendars, scripts, archaeological geometry, and other corpora. Retrieval is a hypothesis generator, not proof.
9. ADVERSARIAL REVIEW — explicitly test mundane explanations: land-use, construction methods, trampling, hoax/fabrication mechanisms, perspective, image artifacts, selection bias, and multiple-comparison effects.
10. REPLAY — rerun from frozen inputs and pinned code/model versions. Record deterministic/non-deterministic conditions.
11. EVIDENCE RECEIPT — bind inputs, hashes, code/model identity, parameters, outputs, tests, controls, reviewer status, and conclusion state.
12. PROMOTION GATE — EXISTS / EXECUTED / REPRODUCED / ESTABLISHED remain distinct. AI agreement alone cannot promote a claim.

CC-specific rule:
A decoded message is HOLD unless the segmentation and mapping survive blinded replication, alternative segmentation, null controls, and independent reproduction. Semantic readings must never be used to tune the geometry model that generated the candidate.

Research targets:
- CCD-9 / Wanborough Plain
- crop-circle geometry families
- glyph/primitive grammar
- vector-field and graph representations
- symmetry and rotational structure
- temporal/astronomical/calendar correspondences
- cross-domain comparisons with ancient inscriptions and archaeological forms

This specification is intentionally agnostic about origin, authorship, intentionality, or non-human hypotheses. Those remain hypotheses to be tested.
