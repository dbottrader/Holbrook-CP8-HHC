# Credits and Collaboration Boundary

## Origin and Stewardship

**Dennis Christie (CP8)** is credited as the originator and steward of the CP8 / ASIN-HHC / Holbrook body of work, including the continuity layer, evidence-boundary framing, repository strategy, provenance discipline, and system-integration direction.

## Collaborator Credit

**Ryan Scott** is credited as a collaborator whose conversations, critiques, adversarial review, and agent-assisted responses contributed to:

- protocol hardening,
- security framing,
- middleware handoff analysis,
- deterministic-runtime requirements,
- critique of insufficient policy-check assumptions,
- clarification of LLM-vs-runtime authority boundaries,
- and refinement of Holbrook / DAR-P admissibility criteria.

## Agent-Generated Material

Outputs from AI agents or assistants, including Ace, Kimi, Grok, Ryan's agents, and other model-generated responses, are not treated as independent authority by default.

They are considered assistant-generated artifacts until reviewed, classified, and attached to evidence receipts under the CP8 evidence ladder.

## Boundary Statement

Crediting collaboration does not collapse authorship, stewardship, or provenance.

The intended attribution model is:

- **Dennis Christie / CP8:** originator, steward, system integrator, evidence-boundary owner.
- **Ryan Scott:** collaborator, critic, adversarial reviewer, and contributor to hardening discussions.
- **AI agents:** assistant-generated artifact producers whose outputs require receipts before authority.

## Working Rule

No artifact gains authority merely because it was generated, named, indexed, or included in a repository.

Authority requires review, receipts, deterministic verification where applicable, and evidence-tier classification.
